About

Hello! I'm Edgar Ferreira, a product engineer from Aracaju, Brazil, dedicated to crafting experiences that connect strategy, design, and technology.

With over 5+ years of experience, From early discovery to launch, I partner with founders and teams to define requirements, prototype flows, and lead implementation across APEX apps, APIs, and data models. I've shipped work for finance, logistics, and media companies, with a stronger focus on the Oracle ecosystem.

Curiosity keeps me multidisciplinary. Whether I'm mapping journeys with UX research, refining handoffs in Figma, or orchestrating delivery with CI/CD and observability, my goal is simple: deliver products that feel intentional and hold up in production — now with a focus on Oracle platforms.
