# Story 2.2: Home page

- Epic: Epic 2: Pages
- Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a visitor, I want the home page to present Edgar, his experience, projects, articles and contact, so that I understand who he is in one scroll.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Hero: profile `heroTitle` split as in the mockup ("Product" / "Engineer"), `heroTagline`, "View Products" button (mockup text) to `#projects`, hero links (`showInHero`) with icons and accessible names.
2. Featured-project carousel (`isFeatured`): card with tags, title, summary, "Read more" to `/project/<slug>/`; prev/next buttons and dots; keyboard operable; `aria-roledescription="carousel"`; works without JS (first slide visible, others reachable as links).
3. `#about`: portrait (+alt), bio Markdown, Technologies & Tools chips, What I Do, Education, Certifications.
4. `#experience`: rows with date range (`Mar 2025 – Present`), duration ("2 years 10 months"), company, role; "Current" for open ranges; `experienceSummary`; "Download CV" link to `/edgar-ferreira-cv.pdf`.
5. `#projects`: every project (sortOrder) with title, tag chips, summary, cover, "Read more"; "View all projects on GitHub" to the GitHub link from profile; project count.
6. `#articles`: cards for every article (newest first) with title, summary, "Read more" to `/article/<slug>/`.
7. `#contact`: name, role · location, phone, email, website, languages, contact links (`showInContact`).
8. Matches `Home — desktop` and `Home — mobile` mockups; Playwright specs per section from fixture content; axe-style a11y basics (headings order, names).
