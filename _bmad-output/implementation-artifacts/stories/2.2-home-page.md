# Story 2.2: Home page

- Epic: Epic 2: Pages
- Status: review

## Story

As a visitor, I want the home page to present Edgar, his experience, projects, articles and contact, so that I understand who he is in one scroll.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Hero: profile `heroTitle` split as in the mockup ("Product" / "Engineer"), `heroTagline`, "View Products" button (mockup text) to `#projects`, hero links (`showInHero`) with icons and accessible names.
2. Featured-project carousel (`isFeatured`): card with tags, title, summary, "Read more" to `/project/<slug>/`; prev/next buttons and dots; keyboard operable; `aria-roledescription="carousel"`; works without JS (first slide visible, others reachable as links).
3. `#about`: portrait (+alt), bio Markdown, Technologies & Tools chips, What I Do, Education, Certifications.
4. `#experience`: rows with date range (`Mar 2025 – Present`), duration ("2 years 10 months"), company, role; "Current" for open ranges; `experienceSummary`; "Download CV" link to `/edgar-ferreira-cv.pdf`.
5. `#projects`: every project (sortOrder) with title, tag chips, summary, cover, "Read more"; "View all projects on GitHub" to the GitHub link from profile; project count.
6. `#articles`: cards for every article (newest first) with title, summary, "Read more" to `/article/<slug>/`.
7. `#contact`: name, role · location, phone, email, website, languages, contact links (`showInContact`).
8. Matches `Home — desktop` and `Home — mobile` mockups; Playwright specs per section from fixture content; axe-style a11y basics (headings order, names).

## Dev notes

Implemented without `/bmad-build` (its loader needs `uv`); followed AGENTS.md directly. Specs written first
and confirmed failing for missing behaviour (Playwright: sections not found; Vitest: modules missing).

**Decisions**
- Hero: the h1 always holds the whole `heroTitle`. Below md it shows both words stacked; from md up the rest
  of the title (split after the first word, `toHeroTitleParts`) is `sr-only` in the h1 and repeated as an
  `aria-hidden` display word on the right, as in the mockup.
- Carousel (`FeaturedProjectsCarousel`, client): without JS the server HTML is a horizontal scroll-snap row
  with the first slide centred and every "Read more" link reachable. After hydration it becomes a
  transform carousel: prev/next buttons, dots (`aria-current`), ArrowLeft/ArrowRight/Home/End,
  `aria-roledescription="carousel"`/`"slide"`, `aria-live="polite"`, and inactive slides `inert` +
  `aria-hidden`. No autoplay. It wraps from first to last and back. A `biome-ignore` with a reason keeps
  `role="group"` on slides (the APG pattern) instead of `<fieldset>`.
- Experience: ranges `Mar 2025 – Present`. Durations count both end months (`Jul 2018 – Apr 2021` =
  `2 years 10 months`), which matches every row of the mockup. Open ranges show "Current" in place of a
  duration, as the mockup does.
- "Read more" pill + arrow circle are one link (`ArrowPillLink`): one tab stop, named
  "Read more about <title>" via an `sr-only` suffix.
- Brand icons: lucide-react 1.x has no brand logos, so `ProfileLinkIcon` maps LinkedIn/GitHub/Facebook/
  Instagram/Twitter to generic glyphs (UserRound, Code, ThumbsUp, Camera, AtSign). The link label is
  always the accessible name.
- Tokens: added the DESIGN.md component literals (`off-white`, `pill`, `pill-hover`, `slide-*`, `code-*`)
  and the shadows (`glow`, `slide`, `cta-inset`) to the `@theme` in `globals.css`. DESIGN.md is unchanged.
- Positional project badges: DESIGN.md mentions four but the mockup shows three (🎨 ⚡ 🚀). The fourth (💡) is
  my choice. Badges cycle and are `aria-hidden`.
- Fixtures: added `seed-second-featured-project` so the carousel has two slides. Updated the story 1.1
  query test for it, plus 1x placeholder PNGs `apps/web/public/uploads/seed-*.png` for every fixture image.
  Updated two older specs that expected the placeholder h1 "Edgar Ferreira": walking skeleton and the
  story 2.1 not-found "links back home" test.

**Deviations**
- Experience rows are not clickable (DESIGN.md › experience-row mentions a `mailto:` row; no AC asks for
  it). Hover styling is kept.
- No section entrance animation (DESIGN.md › Movement). It is optional polish; it can be added later
  behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The carousel shows no left neighbour on the first slide (the mockup shows one on both sides, implying an
  infinite loop).
- The contact phone is plain text, not a `tel:` link: the content has no country code.
